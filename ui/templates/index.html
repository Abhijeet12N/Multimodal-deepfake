    Visual Stream

    Xception Backbone with temporal mean & standard deviation pooling over 8 MTCNN-cropped facial bounding boxes.

    Audio Stream

    Wav2Vec 2.0 Base frozen transformer representations with projection layer over 16 kHz mono waveform.

    Output: 512-dim embedding

    Joint 3D/2D CNN cross-referencing lip ROIs (112×112) against 64-band Log-Mel Spectrograms for audiovisual synchrony.

    Output: 512-dim embedding
    ⚡
